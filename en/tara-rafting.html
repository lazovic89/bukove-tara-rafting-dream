Tara River Rafting

Down Europe's deepest canyon

The Tara River — “The Tear of Europe”

With a canyon up to 1,300 metres deep, the Tara is a true natural wonder. Its turquoise waters, framed by untouched forests and sheer cliffs, offer a rafting experience you won't find anywhere else in Europe.

Safety

Our guests' safety always comes first. All our guides are licensed and have years of experience. We use high-quality equipment that is regularly inspected and maintained. Before every trip, guests receive a short briefing on safety on the water.
