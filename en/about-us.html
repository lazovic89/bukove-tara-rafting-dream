About us

Meet Bukovi Tare

Our story

Bukovi Tare was born out of a love for the Tara River and its magnificent canyon. For more than 15 years we have welcomed guests from across the region and around the world, giving them unforgettable experiences on the water and in nature.

Our camp sits in the heart of a centuries-old beech forest on the banks of the crystal-clear Tara — a UNESCO-protected river flowing through Europe's deepest canyon (1,300 metres deep).

As a small, family-run camp that cares about every guest, we do our best to make each stay unique.

Why Bukovi Tare?
