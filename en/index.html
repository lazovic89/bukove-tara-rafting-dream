Welcome to Bukovi Tare

Set in the heart of the Tara River canyon, our camp offers the perfect blend of adventure and relaxation. Surrounded by centuries-old beech forests and a crystal-clear river, Bukovi Tare is the ideal place for nature lovers and adrenaline seekers alike.

With more than 15 years of experience organizing rafting and hiking trips, our team of professional guides ensures a safe and unforgettable experience for all ages.

Comfortable accommodation

Our rooms and apartments offer the comfort and warmth of a mountain home. Each unit has everything you need for a pleasant stay.

  • Comfortable rooms with private bathroom
  • Family apartments

Home cooking

Enjoy authentic specialities of traditional Bosnian cuisine. All meals are prepared from fresh, local ingredients.

From breakfast with homemade kajmak (clotted cream) and honey to a barbecue dinner on the open terrace overlooking the canyon — every meal is something special.
