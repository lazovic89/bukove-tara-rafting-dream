# Bukovi Tare — Rafting Camp Website

## Overview

A modern, adventure-styled website in Serbian for the Bukovi Tare rafting camp on the Tara River. Bold visuals, dynamic layout, action-oriented feel.

## Design

- **Color palette**: Deep river blue, forest green, orange/amber accents for CTA buttons, dark navy overlays on hero images
- **Typography**: Bold headings (adventure feel), clean body text
- **Layout**: Full-width hero sections, large photos with overlay text, card-based content sections

## Pages & Sections

### 1. Početna (Home)

- **Hero**: Full-screen background image of Tara canyon with bold headline "Bukovi Tare — Avantura na rijeci Tari" + CTA button "Rezervišite"
- **Highlights bar**: 3-4 key stats (km raftinga, godina iskustva, zadovoljnih gostiju)
- **About snippet**: Short intro about the camp with photo
- **Activities preview**: Cards for Rafting + Hiking with images
- **Accommodation preview**: Room/apartment showcase with photos
- **Testimonials**: Guest reviews carousel
- **Contact/Location**: Map embed, phone, Viber/WhatsApp, email

### 2. O nama (About)

- Camp story, team, mission
- Photo gallery of the camp and surroundings
- Why choose Bukovi Tare

### 3. Rafting

- Description of rafting routes (1-day, multi-day options)
- What's included (equipment, guide, transport, food)
- Pricing table
- Safety info
- Photo gallery of rafting action

### 4. Smještaj (Accommodation)

- Rooms/apartments descriptions with photos
- Amenities list
- Traditional food section (domaća kuhinja)

### 5. Aktivnosti (Activities)

- Hiking trails & nature excursions
- Nearby attractions (waterfalls, viewpoints, UNESCO canyon)
- Photo gallery

### 6. Kontakt (Contact)

- Contact form (name, email, phone, message, date picker)
- Phone, Viber/WhatsApp number, email
- Google Maps embed with camp location
- How to get there (directions)

## Navigation

- Sticky header with logo + nav links + phone number + CTA "Rezervišite"
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Footer with quick links, social media, contact info

## Technical

- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Smooth scroll animations
- Images (generator) 
- WhatsApp/Viber click-to-call links
